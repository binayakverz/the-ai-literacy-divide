# Methodology and Reproducibility Documentation

This folder documents the research design, dataset handling, scale construction, analysis families, variable definitions, and interpretation boundaries used in *The AI Literacy Divide: Access, Capability, and Inequality in the Age of Generative AI*.

## Contents
- `METHODOLOGY.md` — research design and analytical approach.
- `REPRODUCIBILITY.md` — dataset version, cleaning audit, analysis record, and reproducibility limitations.
- `DATA_DICTIONARY.csv` — core variables, constructs, and external evidence indicators.

## Important limitation
The manuscript states that no executable analysis-code artifact was present in the supplied materials. These documents therefore provide transparent methodological documentation; they do not claim to provide a complete executable reproduction pipeline. The repository's `tables/` CSVs reproduce manuscript tables, not respondent-level raw data.

All numerical findings and analytical decisions should be checked against the final manuscript in `manuscript/`.
