# Data Sources and Evidence Register

This register organises the datasets and institutional evidence streams used in the manuscript. It does not imply that the sources are harmonised or pooled. The manuscript treats each source according to its own population, unit of analysis, construct, sampling design, and observation period.

## Primary individual-level empirical dataset

- **FITPED AI Literacy Questionnaire data, Version 2** — Figshare dataset. Used for the individual-level analysis of perceived/self-reported AI literacy and related constructs. The dataset contains 1,205 records representing 1,146 unique participants; 59 participant IDs are repeated. It is not a population-representative estimate of AI literacy.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29488523.v2
- **FITPED data article** — Skalka et al. (2025a), *Data in Brief*, 62, 112022.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.112022

## GenAI use and digital access

- **Eurostat dataset `isoc_ai_iaiu`** — Reported use of generative AI by individuals; used for population-level GenAI-use patterns and inequalities.
  - Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.2908/isoc_ai_iaiu
  - Data Browser: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_ai_iaiu/
- **International Telecommunication Union (ITU) DataHub** — Internet use and digital connectivity indicators, including India disaggregation. Used as digital-access context, not as a measure of AI literacy.
  - https://datahub.itu.int/
- **World Bank World Development Indicators, `IT.NET.USER.ZS`** — Individuals using the Internet (% of population). Used as a corroborating Internet-access indicator; it is not an independent respondent-level sample from the ITU series.
  - https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/IT.NET.USER.ZS/~/home

## Digital literacy and capability context

- **UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS)** — Global Framework of Reference on Digital Literacy Skills. Used to contextualise broader digital literacy; not treated as a direct GenAI-literacy measure.
  - Framework PDF: https://www.uis.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/08/ip51-global-framework-reference-digital-literacy-skills-2018-en.pdf
  - UIS Data Browser: https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/
- **OECD Programme for the International Assessment of Adult Competencies (PIAAC), Cycle 2** — Adult skills and digital/problem-solving capability context. Not a GenAI-literacy dataset; India was not among the Round 1 participating countries described in the manuscript.
  - https://www.oecd.org/en/data/datasets/piaac-2nd-cycle-database.html
- **IEA International Computer and Information Literacy Study (ICILS) 2023** — Computer and Information Literacy assessment context. Not a GenAI-literacy dataset.
  - Repository: https://www.iea.nl/data-tools/repository/icils
  - DOI listed in the manuscript's source register: https://doi.org/10.58150/ICILS_2023_data_2

## AI awareness, adoption, and labour-market context

- **Pew Research Center (2025; 2026)** — Cross-national public awareness and views of AI. Used for awareness context, not as a direct AI-literacy or capability assessment. The manuscript's reference list does not provide direct report URLs for these two entries.
- **Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence, AI Index Report 2026** — Aggregate AI adoption and diffusion context, not an individual-level literacy measure.
  - https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report
- **International Labour Organization (ILO), 2025** — Global analysis of potential occupational exposure to generative AI. Exposure is not interpreted as job loss, individual capability, productivity gain, or realised benefit.
  - https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/one-four-jobs-risk-being-transformed-genai-new-ilo–nask-global-index-shows
- **World Bank (2025), Digital Progress and Trends Report 2025: Strengthening AI Foundations** — Broader AI foundations and digital development context.
  - Manuscript reference does not provide a direct report URL.
- **World Bank/Semrush GenAI-adoption evidence** — Aggregate cross-income-group GenAI adoption/traffic context. Traffic measures are not interpreted as representative individual prevalence, demonstrated capability, effective use, or realised benefit.
  - Source PDF: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099856110152535288/pdf/IDU-42736e6b-48fb-45e6-8638-cec68a650f40.pdf

## Emerging objective AI/GenAI capability instruments

These are discussed in the manuscript's literature review as selected-sample assessment research, not as population-wide datasets used for pooled estimation:

- **GLAT** — Generative AI Literacy Assessment Test; Jin et al. (2025). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2025.100436
- **AICOS** — AI Competency Objective Scale; Markus et al. (2025). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2025.100485
- **Objective AI literacy test** — Chiu et al. (2024). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100282

## Comparability and interpretation safeguards

1. The FITPED dataset is the primary individual-level empirical dataset.
2. The other evidence streams are independent aggregate, population, assessment, or contextual sources.
3. No respondent-level observations were merged across FITPED, Eurostat, ITU, WDI, UNESCO, PIAAC, ICILS, Pew, Stanford, ILO, or World Bank/Semrush.
4. Internet access, AI awareness, perceived AI literacy, demonstrated capability, AI use, effective use, and realised benefit are distinct constructs.
5. Country-level or aggregate indicators are not treated as individual-level evidence.
6. The manuscript's conceptual sequence is a measurement architecture, not an empirically demonstrated causal pathway.
