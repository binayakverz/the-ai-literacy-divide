# Step 5 — References and Data Sources

This folder contains:
- `REFERENCES.md` — the manuscript's bibliography, transcribed from the final manuscript.
- `DATA_SOURCES.md` — a structured register of datasets and institutional evidence streams, including their role and interpretation boundaries.

**Important:** These files document the sources as cited in the manuscript. They do not claim that all sources are harmonised, pooled, or independently sampled. Missing URLs have not been invented. For the definitive citation wording, consult the manuscript's References section.
